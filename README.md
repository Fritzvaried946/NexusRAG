## 📄 What is NexusRAG?

NexusRAG is a hybrid system that helps you find information quickly from your documents and knowledge bases. It combines different search methods, like vector search and knowledge graphs, with smart ranking to show the best answers. NexusRAG also reads documents, understands images and tables, and supports a chat interface that streams responses with citations.

It works with modern AI models like Gemini or local Ollama models to give you up-to-date, accurate answers from your own data.

---

## 🎯 Key Features

- **Multi-method search:** Uses vector search and knowledge graphs together.
- **Smart ranking:** Shows the most relevant results first.
- **Document parsing:** Reads and understands different document formats.
- **Visual intelligence:** Captions images and tables in documents.
- **Chat interface:** Allows streaming chat with inline citations.
- **Flexible AI models:** Supports Gemini and Ollama locally.

## 📂 Preparing Your Documents

NexusRAG supports many document types. Make sure your files are in these formats:

- PDFs
- Word documents (`.docx`)
- Text files (`.txt`)
- Excel tables (`.xlsx`)
- Images (`.jpg`, `.png`, `.bmp`) that include charts or tables

For best results, organize your files in folders. You can load multiple files at once in NexusRAG’s interface.

## 🛠 How to Use NexusRAG

### Adding Documents

1. Click on the **Add Documents** button.
2. Select your files or folders.
3. The app will process and parse your documents. This takes a few seconds to a minute depending on file size.

### Searching and Chat

- Enter your question or keywords in the search bar.
- NexusRAG searches using its knowledge graph and vector search to find relevant information.
- It ranks the results and shows them with inline citations.
- Use the chat interface to ask follow-up questions or get explanations. The chat replies update live as the system processes your query.

### Viewing Visual Content

If your documents have images or tables, NexusRAG adds descriptive captions to help you understand the content better.

## 💡 Tips for Best Performance

- Use clear and specific search terms.
- Load documents that are related to the topics you want to explore.
- If you use the local Ollama model, make sure it is installed and configured according to its instructions, found on their official site.
- For cloud Gemini, keep an internet connection active.
- Large document sets may take more time to process initially.
- Restart NexusRAG occasionally to keep it running smoothly.

## 🔧 Troubleshooting

- **App won’t open:** Try restarting your PC or running the app as administrator (right-click > "Run as administrator").
- **No results found:** Check that your documents are loaded and parsed fully. Try different keywords.
- **Slow performance:** Close other programs, especially those using lots of memory.
- **Error messages:** Write down the error and report it on the GitHub Issues page for help.
